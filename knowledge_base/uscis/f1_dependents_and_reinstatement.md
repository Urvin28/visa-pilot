# F-2 dependents and F-1 reinstatement: overview

## F-2 spouse and children

An eligible F-1 student's spouse and unmarried children under 21 may qualify for F-2 classification. Each dependent generally needs their own Form I-20 from the school's DSO and must maintain the dependent status requirements. F-2 dependents are not authorized to work in the United States. Study permission is limited and depends on the dependent's age and whether study is full-time or part-time; check current regulations and consult the DSO before enrolling in a course of study. A dependent who wants to study full-time generally needs to change to an appropriate student status before beginning.

## Reinstatement after falling out of F-1 status

Reinstatement is a complex USCIS process, not an automatic fix. Some students who violated status may be able to ask USCIS to reinstate F-1 status by filing Form I-539, if they satisfy the regulatory criteria. Eligibility and deadlines depend on the reason and duration of the violation, the student's intent and course of study, and other facts. A DSO recommendation does not guarantee USCIS approval. Students should stop and get case-specific guidance promptly; unauthorized employment or a serious/intentional violation can bar reinstatement or have other consequences.

Do not assume that filing a reinstatement application restores F-1 status, allows employment, or makes travel safe while the request is pending. Speak with the DSO and a qualified immigration attorney about the student's particular circumstances before filing or departing.
