# F-1 Optional Practical Training (OPT)

OPT is temporary employment authorization for practical training directly related to an F-1 student's major field of study. OPT is not automatic: eligibility, timing, the DSO's SEVIS recommendation, USCIS adjudication, and the EAD dates all matter.

## Types

- **Pre-completion OPT** takes place before the program end date. It may be part-time while school is in session and full-time during eligible breaks. Time used is deducted from the usual 12-month allotment.
- **Post-completion OPT** takes place after completion of the program. Eligible students may generally receive up to 12 months at each higher educational level, reduced by applicable prior OPT and affected by full-time CPT of 12 months or more at that level.
- Qualifying STEM degree holders may apply for a separate 24-month STEM OPT extension; see the STEM OPT guide.

## Applying for post-completion OPT

1. Ask the DSO to review eligibility and enter the OPT recommendation in SEVIS.
2. Obtain the updated Form I-20 showing the recommendation and check it for accuracy.
3. File Form I-765 in the correct category with USCIS within the filing window. Current Form I-765 instructions say post-completion OPT may be filed up to 90 days before the program end date and no later than 60 days after it; USCIS must receive it within 30 days of the DSO recommendation. Confirm deadlines in the current form instructions and with the DSO.
4. Wait for USCIS approval and the EAD. Do not begin work before the EAD's validity start date.
5. Keep employment directly related to the major and report employment and address changes to the DSO as required.

An application receipt notice is not an EAD and ordinarily does not authorize a new OPT job. Limited automatic work extensions can apply in specifically defined situations; confirm the exact category and current policy rather than assuming an extension applies.

## Employment and unemployment

Post-completion OPT work must be directly related to the major. Students on regular post-completion OPT may generally accrue no more than 90 days of unemployment during the OPT authorization period. Employment must meet the applicable definition and be reported in the required manner through the school/SEVIS process. Ask the DSO how to document the relationship between duties and the major, start/end dates, and any self-employment or multiple-employer arrangements.

## Travel and changes

Travel, transfer to another school, or changing education level may affect an OPT request or authorization. Ask the DSO before taking action. Keep copies of the EAD, Form I-20, passport, visa, and current employment evidence when relevant to travel.
