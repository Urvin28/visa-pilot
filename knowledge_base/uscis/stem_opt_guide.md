# F-1 24-month STEM OPT extension

The STEM OPT extension is a 24-month extension of post-completion OPT for eligible F-1 students. It has additional degree, employer, training-plan, reporting, and timing requirements. Confirm eligibility with the DSO and current USCIS instructions before relying on any deadline.

## Core eligibility points

- The student must be in a valid period of post-completion OPT and have a qualifying bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree in a field on the current DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List. The relevant degree may, in some circumstances, be a previously earned STEM degree from an appropriately accredited and SEVP-certified U.S. institution.
- The job must be directly related to the qualifying STEM degree and meet the training-program requirements.
- The employer must be enrolled in E-Verify and have an employer-employee relationship that meets the rules. The employer must be able to complete and sign Form I-983 and comply with its training and reporting obligations.
- Each qualifying employer must provide at least 20 hours of work per week. Unemployment limits and the cumulative limits for regular OPT plus STEM OPT apply.

## Application process and timing

1. Work with the DSO to confirm the degree/CIP code, employer, and proposed training plan.
2. Complete and sign Form I-983 with the employer; submit it to the DSO as directed. The DSO makes the STEM OPT recommendation in SEVIS and issues an updated Form I-20.
3. File Form I-765 with USCIS in the STEM category in the current filing window. Current instructions generally allow filing up to 90 days before the current OPT EAD expires; a separate deadline applies after the DSO's recommendation is entered. Check the latest I-765 instructions and DSO guidance for the exact filing deadline.
4. If a timely STEM OPT extension application is pending when the current EAD expires, a temporary automatic extension of employment authorization may apply under the regulations, generally for up to 180 days while the application is pending. Verify that the filing and eligibility conditions are met before working under this provision.

## Ongoing duties

- Report required changes to the DSO within the applicable deadlines, including address, employer, employment dates, and material changes to the training plan.
- Complete required validation reports with the DSO, commonly at 6-month intervals, and submit the required I-983 evaluations (including the midpoint and final evaluation) on time.
- Work with the employer to update or replace the I-983 if material training or employer details change. Keep the signed training plan, evaluations, pay records, and evidence that the work relates to the STEM degree.
- Keep total unemployment within the allowed combined limit for OPT and STEM OPT. Contact the DSO if a job ends or a new employer is involved.
